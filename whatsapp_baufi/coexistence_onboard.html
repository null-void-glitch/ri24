Connect a WhatsApp Business App number (Coexistence)

  1. Click Connect with Facebook and log in with the account that owns the business.
  2. Select your existing Business Portfolio and the number that is on the WhatsApp Business App.
  3. On your phone, open WhatsApp Business App → Settings → linked devices / scan QR and scan the QR code shown in the popup to authorize coexistence. The app keeps working; history syncs.
  4. Leave this tab open until you see Success below. Repeat for the second number.
